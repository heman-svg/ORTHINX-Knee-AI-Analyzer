# ORTHINX — Knee AI Analyzer

> Research and prototype platform for automated knee X-ray segmentation, geometric measurement, quality assessment, and structured reporting.

## ⚠️ Research & Prototype Notice

**ORTHINX / Knee AI Analyzer is a research and decision-support prototype. It is not a clinically validated diagnostic system.**

The system does **not** provide:

- Medical diagnosis
- Confirmed osteoarthritis diagnosis or staging
- Kellgren-Lawrence grading
- Treatment recommendations
- Surgical planning
- Total knee arthroplasty recommendations
- Implant sizing

Results must not be used as a substitute for qualified medical assessment.
